Money columns are operational figures in USD from the event stream: Revenue = Seedtag revenue (net paid − post-auction discounts + curator margin); Pub cost = an SSP-side allocation estimate (impression cost + HB net insert cost); Margin = (Revenue − Pub cost) / Revenue. These are the only revenue/cost figures that exist at user-country grain — they do not reconcile with Finance's Publisher Revenue (publisher-reported, EUR, monthly-average FX, publisher grain only). True publisher payout is defined per publisher, never per user country, so splitting it by the viewer's country is a proxy: read Pub cost and Margin as directional only. The Brands view shows revenue from the enriched demand table, a slightly different adjustment basis.
